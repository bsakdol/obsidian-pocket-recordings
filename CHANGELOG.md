## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- First release. Syncs Pocket recordings into conversation and insight notes, with transcripts, summaries, action items, mind maps, and translations.
- Choose what syncs by how far back to look, by Pocket folder, and by Pocket tag.
- Choose how notes are laid out and named, and where they go.
- Handles your edits, moves, deletions, and re-imports, on desktop and mobile. The plugin writes only inside its own region of each note and its own `pocket_*` properties.
